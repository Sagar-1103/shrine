# Startup Hunter — Reference: Verification Checklist

Use this checklist to verify that a discovered startup is **genuine and worth
outreaching to**. A startup must pass at least 4 of 6 checks to be included.

---

## Legitimacy Checks

### ✅ 1. Working Website
- [ ] The company has a live website with a real product or clear landing page
- [ ] The website is not a parked domain, placeholder, or "coming soon" with no substance
- [ ] The website has an About page or Team page

### ✅ 2. Identifiable Founders
- [ ] At least one founder has a LinkedIn profile
- [ ] The founder's LinkedIn shows prior work history (not an empty profile)
- [ ] The founder's employment dates at this company are consistent

### ✅ 3. Public Footprint
- [ ] The startup appears on at least ONE of: Crunchbase, YC, Wellfound, Product Hunt, BetaList
- [ ] OR has been mentioned in tech press (TechCrunch, YourStory, Inc42, etc.)
- [ ] OR has a GitHub organization with real repositories

### ✅ 4. Team Evidence
- [ ] LinkedIn shows at least 2-3 employees beyond the founders
- [ ] OR the company page shows a team section
- [ ] The team size is consistent with what's claimed

### ✅ 5. Product Evidence
- [ ] The product is live and usable, OR in public beta
- [ ] OR there are demo videos, screenshots, or customer testimonials
- [ ] OR the product is on an app store (iOS/Android)

### ✅ 6. Funding / Traction Evidence
- [ ] Funding is listed on Crunchbase or announced publicly
- [ ] OR the startup is part of an accelerator (YC, Techstars, etc.)
- [ ] OR revenue/customer count is mentioned publicly

---

## Red Flags — Exclude the Startup If:

- 🚩 No real humans identifiable as founders
- 🚩 Website is a template with stock photos and no product screenshots
- 🚩 Company claims huge funding but no Crunchbase/press record
- 🚩 LinkedIn company page has 0 followers and 0 employees
- 🚩 Multiple "companies" at the same address with same founder (shell pattern)
- 🚩 Domain registered very recently (< 3 months) with no substance
- 🚩 Asks candidates to pay for applications or training
